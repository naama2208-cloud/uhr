uhr installs from the browser, like an app — on your home screen, full screen, and it opens without a connection.

open uhr
iPhone · Safari
  1. open naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr in Safari
  2. tap share
  3. scroll down, tap add to home screen
  4. tap add — uhr is on your home screen
Android · Chrome
  1. open naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr in Chrome
  2. tap install uhr here — or ⋮ → install app
  3. confirm install
  4. uhr opens from your home screen
