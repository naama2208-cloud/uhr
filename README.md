# ühr

An alarm clock built on Josef Müller-Brockmann's grid: every hour is a square.

- **App (v6):** https://naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr/ — on an iPhone: Share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **The system, taken apart:** https://naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr/tokens.html — move a token, the whole app redraws
- **My log:** https://naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr/log.html — every alarm set, heard, stopped, snoozed, by day
- **Design system:** https://naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr/system.html · **v4:** https://naama2208-cloud.github.io/uhr/v4.html

## v6 — a dynamic grid
- **Zoom:** pinch (or ctrl+scroll / + −) between 2 · 4 · 8 squares a row, like Photos on the iPhone. Squares glide to their new place.
- **Open an hour:** the grid stretches apart around the square until that hour is the screen; closing pulls it back.
- **One week as a week:** today and the six days after it, by name. Scroll on and it rolls round.
- **Create, one step a screen:** time · name · sound · repeat. Swipe left to confirm (on the last step it sets the alarm), swipe right to go back. Only *delete* and *continue*.
- **Four tones** — rise, drop, glass, tick — each one moves the name's letters its own way.
- **Ring:** real while the app is open. The square stretches to the screen; the grid stays. A once-alarm disappears when it's done: no lists.
- **Type:** Helvetica throughout; the logo alone is set in *under*.
